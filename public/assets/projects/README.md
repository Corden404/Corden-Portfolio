## Processing

- The Qwen, YOLO, thesis, and One World images are copied without changing their bytes or content.
- The MaoGai promotional image was resized proportionally from 1792 × 2400 to 896 × 1200 and encoded as WebP at quality 86. No crop, retouching, or added content was applied.
- The five published images total 1,039,690 bytes.
- No cover was added for SMP2026 Starnet Agent: the public repository did not contain a suitable screenshot or diagram in its committed image assets when inspected.
